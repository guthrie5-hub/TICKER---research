# Partner Exchange — Adobe and Microsoft

## Question 1 — Selection and evidence

**Question:** Why did you choose Microsoft, and what evidence supports an important assumption in your forecast?

**Partner answer:** Microsoft is a large, established public company with detailed disclosures and multiple major segments, providing enough history for a pro forma, DCF, peer comparison, and sensitivity analysis. The partner used Microsoft’s FY2026 10-K as the primary historical source.

**Evidence check:** I opened Microsoft’s [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm). It supports the detailed-disclosure claim and reports Microsoft Cloud revenue of $214.4bn, Azure and other cloud-services growth of 41%, and cloud-margin pressure from AI-infrastructure investment.

## Question 2 — Model and valuation

**Question:** Why are your DCF valuation and peer valuation different?

**Partner answer:** The DCF uses forecast cash flows, WACC, and terminal growth, while peer valuation reflects market pricing of comparable companies relative to earnings. The partner’s base FCFF DCF was about $197.60 per diluted share and peer result was about $371.30; they did not average them.

**Check:** $371.30 − $197.60 = **$173.70 per share**. The arithmetic difference is correct, but the specific FCFF, WACC, terminal-growth, peer, and share-count inputs were not provided, so the full model trace remains unresolved.

## Question 3 — Sensitivity and interpretation

**Question:** Does the highest-ranked sensitivity driver mean it is Microsoft’s biggest risk?

**Partner answer:** No. The ranking shows the largest model effect over the selected input ranges, not probability, uncertainty, or the likelihood that the assumption changes.

**Evidence check:** Supported. The ranking could change if the tested ranges change.

## Follow-up

**Question:** What exact Microsoft revenue-growth assumption did you use, and which FY2026 10-K or guidance disclosure supports that number rather than only the general Azure/AI story?

**Answer / gap:** The partner identified this as a research item but did not provide the numerical assumption or exact source locator. The unresolved work is to connect Azure/AI growth, infrastructure spending, margin, capex, and cash-flow evidence to the forecast.

## Summary

Microsoft remains watch–defer. Azure/AI growth can increase revenue and cash flow, but infrastructure investment can pressure margins and capital spending.
