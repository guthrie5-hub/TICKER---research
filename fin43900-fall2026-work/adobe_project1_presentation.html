Video 1 · Research evolution

Company, decision user, and evidence

Decision question

Should a buy-side fund with no Adobe position initiate, watch/defer, or not initiate at $238.93 per share?

Why Adobe

Adobe sells subscription software for creative work, documents, and customer experience. Its recurring revenue and operating profitability make FCFF analysis appropriate.

Primary evidence

FY2025 10-K supplies the audited forecast base. The Q3 FY2026 release supplies updated revenue guidance. The Q3 FY2026 10-Q supplies cash, debt, and shares for the valuation bridge.

What changed

Early work had separate FCFE and DCF pieces. The final system links five years of statements to FCFF and an enterprise-to-equity bridge.

Video 3 · Committee action

Initiate, with conditions

Why initiate

The downside DCF value of $279.39 and base value of $328.09 exceed the $238.93 market price.

The conclusion relies on projected revenue growth and operating margins that generate FCFF. Revenue growth alone does not establish value.

Why remain cautious

71.8% of enterprise value occurs after FY2030. A 12% WACC reduces value to $257.25 per share.

The reverse DCF reaches the market price with a −7.72 percentage-point shift to each FY2027–FY2030 growth rate, holding other inputs fixed.

Video 3 · Monitoring and reversal

Evidence that could change the decision

Monitor

  • ARR and subscription-revenue growth
  • GAAP operating margin
  • AI-related cost and reinvestment disclosures
  • Updated WACC and peer evidence

Reconsider or defer if

  • FY2026 revenue misses the $26.601bn base
  • GAAP margin falls materially below the approximately 35% base
  • ARR or subscription growth makes the downside case look too optimistic
  • The market price exceeds the supported range before operating evidence improves
