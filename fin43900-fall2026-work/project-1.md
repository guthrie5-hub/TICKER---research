# Adobe Inc. (NASDAQ: ADBE) — Initial Company Research Report

**Valuation date:** November 28, 2025 (fiscal year-end reporting date)  
**Primary source:** Adobe Inc., Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November 28, 2025, filed January 15, 2026, SEC accession no. 0000796343-26-000003. [Source: Form 10-K, cover page; SEC EDGAR filing detail.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

## Business snapshot

Adobe provides Digital Media products and services including Creative Cloud applications, Acrobat, Adobe Express, and Adobe Firefly. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

For fiscal 2025, Adobe reported total revenue of $23.769 billion, including $22.904 billion of subscription revenue, and operating income of $8.706 billion. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 48.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

Adobe’s business is subscription-led and operating-profitable. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 48.]

## Operating driver

Management identified Digital Media annualized recurring revenue (ARR) as the key metric used to assess the health and trajectory of its Digital Media segment. Adobe defines Digital Media ARR as the annual value of Digital Media subscriptions and services plus the annual value of Digital Media Enterprise Term License Agreements. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

Digital Media ARR was $19.20 billion at November 28, 2025, representing 11.5% year-over-year growth; Digital Media revenue was $17.65 billion in fiscal 2025, up 11% from fiscal 2024. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

The preliminary investment view treats ARR growth as a leading indicator to test, rather than proof of durable future revenue and cash-flow growth. Management’s use of Digital Media ARR to assess the segment’s health supports its relevance, but not that inference by itself. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

## Capital structure and share evidence

Adobe reported $5.431 billion of cash and cash equivalents at November 28, 2025. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 47.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

Total senior-notes principal outstanding was $6.150 billion at that date. The reported long-term debt carrying value was $6.210 billion; the difference reflects the fair value of interest-rate swaps and unamortized discount and debt-issuance costs. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 47; Note 17, p. 82.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

Adobe used 427.0 million diluted weighted-average shares to calculate fiscal-2025 diluted EPS. This is a period-average EPS measure, while the balance sheet reports 413 million common shares outstanding at November 28, 2025. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 47–48; Note 15, p. 79.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

For any subsequent enterprise-to-equity bridge, I will use one consistent debt convention and point-in-time shares outstanding rather than diluted weighted-average shares. This is an analytical choice based on the distinct debt and share measures reported in the filing. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 8, pp. 47–48; Note 15, p. 79; Note 17, p. 82.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

## Investment case and key open questions

The initial view is that Adobe has recurring-revenue growth and substantial operating profitability. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33; Item 8, p. 48.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

The unresolved questions are whether AI-related product adoption and monetization can sustain ARR growth, whether competitive pressure can compress margins, and whether the market price implies an attractive expected return. These are analytical questions prompted by management’s AI-adoption strategy and the reported ARR growth, not claims that the outcomes will occur. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

My next research steps are to build a multi-year history of ARR, revenue, operating margin, and free cash flow; establish market capitalization at the selected valuation date; and test downside cases for lower growth and margin compression.

## Call

**Watch - Defer.** The available evidence shows 11.5% Digital Media ARR growth and substantial operating profitability, but it does not yet establish valuation upside or the durability of AI-related monetization. [Source: Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 33; Item 8, p. 48.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)

This call would change if evidence shows that Digital Media ARR growth is not durable or cannot be sustained without material margin compression. The ARR trend and operating-income baseline are the measures I will monitor. ## Source log and prior-plan check

### FY2023 Form 10-K

- **Filing:** Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 1, 2023; filed January 17, 2024; accession no. 0000796343-24-000006. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634324000006/adbe-20231201.htm)
- **Management’s stated plan:** Adobe said it expected Creative Cloud to drive sustained long-term revenue growth by expanding its customer base with products including Adobe Express and Firefly, and by delivering generative-AI capabilities to existing customers. [Source: FY2023 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 37.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634324000006/adbe-20231201.htm)
- **What happened next:** In fiscal 2024, Creative revenue grew 10% to $12.68 billion, Document Cloud revenue grew 18% to $3.18 billion, and Digital Media ARR grew 13% to $17.33 billion. These outcomes are consistent with continued growth, but the filing does not isolate the causal effect of Firefly or Express. [Source: FY2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 41.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634325000004/adbe-20241129.htm)

### FY2024 Form 10-K

- **Filing:** Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November 29, 2024; filed January 13, 2025; accession no. 0000796343-25-000004. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634325000004/adbe-20241129.htm)
- **Management’s stated plan:** Adobe described a focus on product innovation and on driving adoption and usage of AI-powered solutions while growing subscription revenue. [Source: FY2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 39.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634325000004/adbe-20241129.htm)
- **What happened next:** Fiscal-2025 Digital Media ARR grew 11.5% to $19.20 billion, Digital Media revenue grew 11% to $17.65 billion, and Digital Experience revenue grew 9% to $5.86 billion. Adobe also reported AI innovation across Digital Media solutions. This supports operational execution of the stated plan, but does not prove that AI alone caused the growth. [Source: FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 33–34.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)
