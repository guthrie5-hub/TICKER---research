## Company and evidence

Adobe primarily sells subscription software for creative work, documents, and customer experience. Its Q3 FY2026 release reported $6.76bn revenue, $27.50bn total Adobe ARR, and $2.35bn GAAP operating income; it also raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $26.576bn–$26.626bn. The full source map, dates, filing locators, peer policy, and every load-bearing assumption are in [adobe_project1_sources.md](adobe_project1_sources.md).

The model begins with FY2025 audited statements and forecasts FY2026E–FY2030E. It links income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow; every forecast year prints a zero balance-sheet gap, a cash-flow-to-balance-sheet cash tie, and cash above the $1.0bn floor. The valuation-date enterprise-to-equity bridge uses the August 2026 10-Q cash/investments and debt balances with September 18 shares outstanding, rather than an EPS weighted-average share count.

The DCF and EV/revenue methods both value Adobe above the saved market price under their stated inputs. They are not averaged: the DCF depends on Adobe’s modeled margins, reinvestment, WACC, and terminal assumptions; EV/revenue reflects the market’s relative pricing of two only partially comparable software companies. The P/E output is retained to meet the equity-multiple requirement but withheld from the committee range because the annual EPS periods are not sufficiently aligned.

## What the market price implies

Holding FY2026 revenue guidance, margins, taxes, reinvestment, debt schedule, buybacks, WACC, terminal growth, cash/debt bridge, and shares fixed, a reverse DCF reaches the $238.93 market price with a **−7.72 percentage-point shift** to each FY2027–FY2030 revenue-growth rate. This is one internally consistent set of assumptions, not proof that the market is wrong or that the shares are mispriced.

## Sensitivity and downside link

The named downside case is **AI/competition downside**: lower post-FY2026 revenue growth and operating margin produce FY2030 EBIT of $10,783.1m, FCFF of $9,096.9m, and $279.39 per share. In the base case, FY2030 EBIT is $12,715.9m, FCFF is $10,870.7m, and value is $328.09 per share. The causal path is revenue and gross margin → EBIT → after-tax operating profit and reinvestment → FCFF → enterprise value.

The model’s WACC/growth grid verifies the expected directions: value rises when terminal growth rises and falls when WACC rises. The conclusion does not treat a sensitivity span as likelihood; it shows which assumptions the model is most exposed to over those particular ranges.

## Committee action, monitoring, and reversal

**Initiate** because the linked DCF downside value of $279.39 per share and base value of $328.09 exceed the September 24, 2026 market price of $238.93. The causal claim is not merely that revenue rises: the forecast assumes revenue growth and operating margins that produce higher FCFF. Reconsider or defer if reported FY2026 results fail to support the $26.601bn revenue base or roughly 35% GAAP operating margin, if the price rises above the supported valuation range before the evidence improves, or if refreshed WACC/peer evidence removes the margin of safety.

**Monitor:** Adobe’s next reported total ARR/subscription-revenue growth, GAAP operating margin, and AI-related cost/reinvestment disclosures. Those items can test the revenue and margin assumptions directly.
